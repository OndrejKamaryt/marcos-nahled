Story

Between the gym and the track

Marcos Pier trains as a hybrid athlete: strength work and running in the same week, in dark gyms and on open tracks. Alongside the training, Marcos coaches online and offers three training programmes.

How the levels build

Level 1 builds an aerobic base and puts squat, hinge, press and pull in place over a six-day week. Level 2 adds sled and erg work and running under fatigue for Hyrox. Level 3 peaks with double sessions and advanced periodisation.

Training

Three levels, one system

Downloadable PDF programmes, six days on and one off each week. Each level builds on the one before: Level 1 lays the base, Level 2 prepares the race, Level 3 peaks.

  • Level 1

    6 to 8 weeks

    Hybrid Base Builder

    €60

    Start hybrid training: you lift or you run, not yet both.

    Six days on, one off

    See Level 1 on Payhip: Hybrid Base Builder, opens in a new tab

    Inside the week

    Three full-body strength sessions, two runs (one short and hard, one long and easy) and one easy long bike day.

    By the end: used to six days a week, an aerobic base, and squat, hinge, press and pull in place.

  • Level 2

    8 to 10 weeks

    Hyrox Prep System

    €110

    Prepare for a race, or you already train and feel stuck.

    Six days on, one off

    See Level 2 on Payhip: Hyrox Prep System, opens in a new tab

    Inside the week

    Two upper-body sessions and one lower-body session, each with a row or ski erg finisher. A threshold run, an easy shakeout run, an easy bike day, and a Hyrox-style compromised session to close the week.

    By the end: an engine that holds under fatigue and a body rehearsed for race day.

  • Level 3

    10 to 12 weeks

    Hybrid Peak Protocol

    €160

    For serious athletes, with four double-session days.

    Six days on, one off, four double-session days

    See Level 3 on Payhip: Hybrid Peak Protocol, opens in a new tab

    Inside the week

    Three upper-body sessions and one leg day, each with a run or erg finisher. An easy bike day, a bike and tempo run day, and a compromised session with carries to close the week. On the four double days, the two sessions are four to five hours apart.

Bars are weeks on a scale of twelve: solid to the shortest run, lighter to the longest. Squares are one week: filled for training, empty for the day off, split for a double session. The squares show the mix of days, not their order.

What equipment do I need?

Level 1: dumbbells, barbells, a cable machine, a pull-up bar, leg curl and leg extension machines, and a bike. Levels 2 and 3 add an incline treadmill, ski and row ergs, a sled with plates, and a wall ball.

Motion

  • Hero. One moment, on load: the photo settles from 104 % to 100 % in 600 ms. The name and the line are simply there. Built.
  • Header. Stays at the top; carbon over the hero, graphite once the page scrolls. Built where the browser supports scroll-driven animation; graphite elsewhere.
  • Training. The hovered or focused level turns midnight blue and an orange rule grows across its top; a level opened from a link shows the same state. “Inside the week” and the questions open instantly. Built.
  • Content. Once real posts are in, each card links to its post; on hover or focus the cover brightens a step and the title underlines. No scale, no tilt. Described.
  • Touch. No hover on a phone. A level opened from a link shows the selected state, and the post row scrolls sideways with snap points. Built.
  • Everything else. No reveal on every section, no parallax, no autoplay. A future video shows a poster and plays on request.
  • Reduced motion. Nothing moves; everything shows in its final state.

Layers that switch on later

  • Each section is a self-contained block with its own anchor and an optional navigation item. Adding or removing a layer is a setting, not a redesign.
  • This draft shows every layer so you can judge it. At launch, Athlete stays off until a result is confirmed, and Content joins the navigation once the first posts are picked.
  • Coaching shows its status and gets its own application only when it opens.
  • Projects stays hidden, in the navigation and on the page, until there is something public to show. The slot above is what switches on.
  • The Currently fields are label and value pairs: any can be renamed, added or dropped.
